# Quantitative and mixed-methods UX research

**I'm a UX researcher who does the quantitative work most mixed-methods teams borrow a data scientist for, and the qualitative work most quant researchers hand off.**
{: .lede}

PhD in social and personality psychology (UBC, 2024). Eighteen months as the quantitative specialist on Google's Android Auto UX Research team, on contract. Now a contract quantitative UX researcher at Meta, working on global brand-awareness surveys. Based in San Francisco, and open to full-time quantitative and mixed-methods UX research roles.

## Three things I do well

- **Design studies backward from the decision.** Start from the choice the team has to make, work back to the result format, the analysis, the sample, and only then the instrument. It is how two vague asks became one survey that changed how an engineering team prioritizes bugs.
- **Make qualitative data count.** Codebooks with rules, two coders on every cell, blind adjudication, a human check, and a quote behind every number. 6,504 open-ended responses coded before a launch, with an audit trail a qualitative researcher would accept.
- **Say what the evidence can and cannot support.** Weighted inference instead of raw means, penalties instead of averages when the decision is about failures, and the limits stated before anyone asks.

## Selected work

<div class="cards" markdown="1">

### [Benchmarking an in-car assistant: from bug frequency to bug cost](/work/assistant-benchmarking/)
A ~1,000-driver survey, designed backward from the decisions, that changed how Engineering prioritizes assistant bugs. Factor analysis, Elastic Net, Shapley importance, penalty-reward asymmetry.

### [Coding 6,504 open-ended responses with an audit trail](/work/coding-open-ends/)
A pre-launch study of privacy controls: 108 + 20 unmoderated think-alouds and an AI-assisted coding pipeline with dual coding, κ .75–.87, blind adjudication, and a 92% human check. Changed onboarding copy before launch.

### [Rebuilding an annual satisfaction benchmark so partner rankings could be trusted](/work/satisfaction-benchmark/)
About 1,800 drivers across seven car brands. Post-stratification weighting to fleet share, inference instead of means, LASSO key-driver analysis. Partner strategy shifted from new features to reliability.

### [Where attitudes and behavior diverge: 300,000 posts and panel surveys](/work/attitudes-vs-behavior/)
Doctoral research published in Psychological Science (2024). API collection, NLP classification validated against human coding, and a survey experiment that explained why divisive content wins.
