# Work

Each case study follows the same shape: the decision at stake, what I did, what changed, and what I would do differently. The three Google studies describe decisions, methods, and sample sizes; they leave out metric values, partner and competitor names, unreleased features, and internal tools, and their figures are illustrative. The doctoral and volunteer work is fully public.

<div class="cards" markdown="1">

### [Benchmarking an in-car assistant: from bug frequency to bug cost](/work/assistant-benchmarking/)
<span class="tag">Google · Android Auto</span><span class="tag">survey design</span><span class="tag">key-driver analysis</span><span class="tag">penalty-reward</span>
Two asks, one instrument, about 1,000 drivers across three panels. Engineering adopted the resulting impact × frequency table to prioritize bugs by what they cost satisfaction, not how often they are reported.

### [Coding 6,504 open-ended responses with an audit trail](/work/coding-open-ends/)
<span class="tag">Google · Android Auto</span><span class="tag">think-alouds</span><span class="tag">qualitative coding</span><span class="tag">LLM-assisted</span>
Pre-launch study of privacy controls for a personalization feature. 108 + 20 unmoderated sessions; dual independent coding, blind adjudication, a 92% human check. Onboarding copy changed before launch.

### [Rebuilding an annual satisfaction benchmark so partner rankings could be trusted](/work/satisfaction-benchmark/)
<span class="tag">Google · Android Auto</span><span class="tag">weighting</span><span class="tag">LASSO</span><span class="tag">partner readouts</span>
About 1,800 drivers, seven car brands. Post-stratification to real fleet share, design-based inference, key-driver analysis, and per-partner readouts that paired numbers with drivers' words.

### [Where attitudes and behavior diverge: 300,000 posts and panel surveys](/work/attitudes-vs-behavior/)
<span class="tag">PhD · UBC</span><span class="tag">behavioral logs</span><span class="tag">NLP</span><span class="tag">survey experiment</span>
Most people say they like political open-mindedness; closed-minded posts win online. Behavioral data plus a segment-level survey explained the gap. Psychological Science, 2024.

## Also

**Segmenting users to find interface friction (freelance, 2025).** For The Joint Chiropractic, I designed custom survey instruments and modeled the responses in R, using latent profile analysis to find data-driven user segments and isolate where the platform interface created friction. Worked with design and engineering on targeted accessibility and interface changes; bug and support reports fell 64% after the changes shipped.

**Smaller studies at Google that changed decisions.** A matched comparison of two digital-car-key types from in-product surveys, restricted to car-phone pairs that supported both, which changed the recommendation to partners. Analysis lead for a within-subject driving-simulator experiment (N = 14; Detection Response Task telemetry; mixed-effects models) on assistant displays and cognitive load. Sixteen cognitive interviews on the team's core survey metrics that cut redundant items from an expensive survey and seeded an SEM/IRT validation plan.
